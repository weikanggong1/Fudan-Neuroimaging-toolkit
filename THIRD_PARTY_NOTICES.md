The PyTorch code was supplied as a user-provided implementation bundle and integrated with modifications; the official HDF5 weights and AtlasMesh files remain external. The newer thalamus and hippocampus/amygdala paths have real-data comparisons but have not met every-label acceptance. See `validation/subregions/README.md` and `validation/subregions/nuclei.md`.
